# DIRECTOR QUALITY REVIEW — B1-B5 Evidence

## B1: 戏剧理解 (Dramatic Understanding)

**Evidence**: All 4 scenes correctly identify and render their core dramatic change — power transfer through physical objects.

- **Scene 1 (S1-1/S1-2/S1-3)**: Interrogation power reversal. Director structures three shots around the photo→badge exchange. S1-1 establishes the interrogation stance (photo push, question). S1-2 isolates Xu's calm badge retrieval — the power flip moment. S1-3 returns to two-shot showing both objects on the desk, Lin's hand withdrawn. The dramatic arc from Lin-dominant to neutralized is rendered through object placement and body posture changes, not dialogue alone.

- **Scene 2 (S2-1/S2-2/S2-3)**: Silent grief and transfer of agency. Director uses the kettle whistle as dramatic pivot — Zhou Cheng refuses to answer the direct question but offers a key after the whistle. S2-1 wide two-shot establishes kitchen geography and tension. S2-2 isolates Zhou Cheng's face during the accusation — steam as silent witness. S2-3 returns to two-shot for the key handoff and letter left behind.

- **Scene 3 (S3-1/S3-2/S3-3)**: Mutual negotiation under time pressure. Director uses the tunnel entry/exit as a dramatic clock. S3-1 establishes command and approaching darkness. S3-2 isolates the storage card in Mi Ya's fingers during total tunnel darkness — the moment of maximum leverage. S3-3 reveals the resolution as light returns.

- **Scene 4 (S4-1/S4-2/S4-3/S4-4)**: Concealment through movement. Director structures the chase as a concealment operation — S4-1 establishes pursuit geometry. S4-2 (half-second nodes) is the dramatic peak: envelope slides under truck. S4-3 confirms pursuers' ignorance from their perspective. S4-4 provides release — breathing, engine sound, envelope secure.

## B3: 跨域统一 (Cross-Domain Unity)

**Evidence**: Visual strategy is consistent across four independent dramatic situations while adapting to each setting.

- **Light as dramatic marker**: Scene 1 (single cold fluorescent, static), Scene 2 (mixed warm stove + cool window, dynamic light ratio shift at kettle-off), Scene 3 (dual-source warm lamp + cold passing lights, tunnel transition arcs), Scene 4 (cold fluorescent in parking garage, wet reflections, three-zone concealment lighting). Each scene's light design serves its dramatic function while using the same cinematic language.
- **Object as power locus**: Every scene centers a physical object on a horizontal surface — desk, table, narrow table, and ground (under truck). The camera consistently frames the object's spatial relationship to the characters.
- **Hand as decision organ**: Across all 4 scenes, the Director consistently uses hand actions as dramatic pivots — Lin's hand withdrawing from photo, Zhou Lan's hand taking key, Mi Ya's fingers releasing storage card, A-Ze's hands sliding envelope under truck.

## B4: 全片弧线 (Full-Episode Arc)

**Evidence**: The four independent scenes form a coherent thematic progression.

- **Visual rhythm arc**: Cold confrontation (S1) → warm grief (S2) → tense negotiation in transit (S3) → kinetic release (S4). The scenes move from static interiors to dynamic exterior, from seated to running.
- **Object scale progression**: Tiny badge → small key → fingernail-sized storage card → large document envelope. The objects grow in physical size while the stakes shift from personal power to survival.
- **Light arc**: Cold fluorescent (S1) → warm mixed (S2) → warm/cold dual-source with dynamic change (S3) → cold fluorescent with rain and motion (S4). Temperature and complexity increase through the episode.
- **Episode Review confirmed**: All cross-scene transitions are correctly documented as hard cuts between independent situations. No continuity conflicts.

## B5: 场面调度 (Mise-en-Scène)

**Evidence**: Spatial design is fully executable within each scene's physical constraints.

- **Scene 1**: 3m×4m interrogation room. Director positions three cameras at measurable angles (center-right 15°, left-front 10°, center) at seated eye height. The metal desk as central stage, single overhead fluorescent as sole light source. No spatial impossibilities.
- **Scene 2**: Kitchen with sink-right, table-left, stove-center-rear. Three depth planes (foreground counter, mid table, background stove/window). Zhou Lan's path: standing at sink → reaching across table for key. Zhou Cheng's path: seated throughout, hand extends key forward. Window light + stove fire as two anchored sources.
- **Scene 3**: Train compartment 2m×2.5m, narrow table center. Two fixed seats. Window behind with passing city lights. Wall lamp as fixed warm source. Tunnel transition: 2.0s darken → total dark → 3.0s return. All camera positions from front within 1m depth.
- **Scene 4**: Multi-level parking garage. Ramp with drainage ditch, turning corner, parked truck (4-5m, 30-40cm clearance). A-Ze's path: up ramp → over ditch → crouch at truck corner → slide envelope under → continue up → roof edge. Pursuers' path: up ramp → past truck without stopping → continue up. Four camera positions (28mm wide tracking, 24mm low-angle, 50mm fixed at truck front, 40mm behind at railing) all spatially coherent.
