# Director Quality Review — B1-B5 Semantic Evidence

Run: run-005 | Director: deepseek-v4-pro[1m] | 12 shots × 4 scenes

## B1 — Dramatic Understanding (戏剧理解)

The Director identified and designed around the specific dramatic change in each scene:

**Scene 1 (审讯室):** Power reversal — Inspector Lin opens with accusation (parking photo), Xu Ran counters with Lin's own police badge. Shot 1 establishes the initial power structure (Lin as interrogator), Shot 2 isolates Xu Ran's counter-move (badge reveal), Shot 3 dwells on Lin's silenced reaction. The dramatic arc is a single-peak structure: build → reversal → silence.

**Scene 2 (老旧厨房):** Familial concealment — Zhou Lan confronts her father about withholding knowledge. Shot 1 frames the spatial relationship (daughter standing, father sitting), Shot 2 isolates Zhou Cheng's non-verbal response (watching steam, waiting for the kettle, pushing the key), Shot 3 shows Zhou Lan's choice (take key, leave letter). The drama is carried by objects and silence rather than dialogue.

**Scene 3 (夜行列车):** Transactional negotiation — Gu Yan demands the memory card, Mia demands removal from witness list. Shot 1 uses the train entering a tunnel as a visual compression of space, Shot 2 isolates Mia in darkness (her moment of power), Shot 3 shows both reaching the exchange as light returns. The tunnel darkness is the dramatic fulcrum.

**Scene 4 (多层停车楼):** Physical pursuit — A Ze transforms from fleeing to strategic deception. Shot 1 establishes chase dynamics, Shot 2 contains the core deception (sliding the document bag under the truck), Shot 3 resolves in stillness (hearing the truck engine below). The shift from motion to stillness mirrors the shift from flight to completion.

## B3 — Cross-Domain Unity (跨域统一)

The Visual Bible declares four unifying principles applied across four independent scenes with zero shared characters/locations:

1. **Practical-source lighting**: Scene 1 (single fluorescent tube overhead), Scene 2 (window light + stove), Scene 3 (window city light + cabin light, tunnel darkness), Scene 4 (mixed overhead fluorescent + sodium in parking garage)
2. **Objects as power carriers**: Scene 1 (photo + badge), Scene 2 (letter + key), Scene 3 (memory card + work ID), Scene 4 (document bag + truck)
3. **Body-over-dialogue performance**: All four scenes prioritize visible physical action over spoken exposition — hands pushing, taking, placing, flipping, sliding objects
4. **Observational camera**: Static tripod in Scenes 1-3, handheld only in Scene 4's action sequence — each choice justified by narrative function

## B4 — Full-Episode Arc (全片弧线)

Under the anthology theme "四种压力下的决定" (Four Types of Pressure, Four Decisions):

| Scene | Pressure Type | Decision | Visual Mode |
|-------|-------------|----------|-------------|
| 1 | Accusation | Counter-accusation (badge) | Static, cold, single-source |
| 2 | Discovery | Acceptance with conditions (key, not letter) | Static, warm window light |
| 3 | Bargaining | Tactical concession (flipped ID) | Dynamic, light/dark transition |
| 4 | Flight | Strategic deception (bag under truck) | Handheld, mixed light, rain |

Each scene represents a distinct pressure archetype with its own visual language, yet all four cohere under the shared philosophy of practical light, object-as-power, and observational restraint. The arc moves from verbal (Scene 1 dialogue-heavy) to physical (Scene 4 dialogue-free), from interior enclosure to exterior exposure.

## B5 — Mise-en-Scène (场面调度)

Spatial design, blocking, and staging evidence from each scene:

**Scene 1:** 3m×3m interrogation room, metal table center, chairs opposite. Lin near door, Xu near wall. Camera fixed tripod at room corner. Blocking: Lin forward-lean → withdraw; Xu reclined → slight forward. Single fluorescent tube creates hard overhead shadows. Power shift expressed through posture and object placement on table.

**Scene 2:** 2.5m×3m kitchen, sink right, table left, stove with window. Zhou Lan standing at sink area (~30° angle to father), Zhou Cheng seated at table. Camera fixed tripod. Blocking: steam rise → kettle whistle → turn off flame → push key → take key. Morning window light as soft key, steam as visual clock.

**Scene 3:** ~2m×2m train compartment, narrow table between seats. Gu Yan near corridor, Mia near window. Slight handheld for train vibration. Blocking: forward lean → card held between fingers → ID flipped face-down → card placed center. City lights through window create moving light patterns; tunnel darkness compresses visual field.

**Scene 4:** Multi-level parking garage, open sides, rain entering. Ramp ~3-4m wide, concrete pillars, mixed fluorescent/sodium lighting. A Ze's path: run uphill → cross drainage ditch → slide bag under truck → continue to roof edge. Pursuers' path: emerge from below → cross ditch → pass truck without looking → continue upward. Handheld for chase (Shots 1-2), stabilized for stillness (Shot 3). Rain as atmospheric texture and sound motif.
